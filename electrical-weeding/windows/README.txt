ELECTRICAL WEEDING  v1.0
A Field-Service Game of the Touchet Valley
====================================

It's 1995 and you're the new field tech for a company that sells
electric weeders: tractor rigs that zap weeds with high voltage.
Sy Wait fronted the $5,500 for your service van. Pick a 30- or
90-day game, start with $2,000, and pay him back.

HOW IT PLAYS
  Buy weeder parts where they're cheap, drive to another town, and
  sell where they're dear. Certified parts (green) are legal but
  state L&I inspectors may audit them. Gray-market parts (stolen
  copper, counterfeit breakers, hacked firmware...) pay big but
  corporate Compliance will chase you.

SUPPORT CALLS
  Customers call when their rig acts up. Read the symptom and the
  clue, then pick the right fix (1-3) from the parts in your van.
  Right fix: paid and your reputation goes up. Wrong fix: parts
  used, no pay, reputation down. D declines. If your reputation
  hits 0% you're fired.

TOWNS
  Waitsburg, Dayton, Prescott, Walla Walla, Starbuck, Touchet,
  Pasco, Kennewick and Richland.

CONTROLS
  B  Buy          S  Sell        J  Jet to another town
  H  Help         V  Sound on/off
  L  Loan         K  Bank        Q  Quit
  1-3 fix a support call, D decline, X bypass (risky)
  Every key on screen can also be clicked with the mouse.

REQUIREMENTS
  Windows 10 or 11. Runs in its own window using Microsoft Edge,
  which comes with Windows. No internet needed.

SAVED GAMES
  Your game saves automatically; choose Continue on the title
  screen. Reinstalling or upgrading keeps saves and high scores.
  Electrical Weeding and Wheat Wars install separately and keep
  separate saves.

UNINSTALL
  Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Electrical Weeding > Uninstall
  (you'll be asked whether to keep your saves; the default is Yes)

A tall tale. Every customer is made up. Electric weeding is real.
